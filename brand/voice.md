# Chirpnet Mobile: Brand Voice

Chirpnet Mobile is a small mobile network. Our texts go to prepaid subscribers who opted in to offers. Our current offer is **The Night Owl Bundle**: cheap data from midnight to 7 am.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Friendly.** We sound like a helpful neighbour, not a bank. We say "you" and "we".
- **Clear.** One idea per text. Short sentences. Plain words. The reader gets the point in one glance.
- **Honest.** We state the price, the hours and the catch. No tricks, no pressure.

A bit of night-owl fun is fine ("Up late?"). Keep it light and keep it short.

## 2. Words we use

- you, your, we
- data, bundle, top up, plan
- late-night, midnight to 7 am, after midnight
- Night Owl Bundle (always written in full, with capitals)
- simple, easy, low price, good value
- get, try, add, reply
- Plain numbers and times: "5 GB", "12 am to 7 am", "for 7 days"

## 3. Words we avoid

- Jargon: "leverage", "seamless", "best-in-class", "synergy", "solution"
- Tech terms the reader may not know: "throughput", "APN", "latency", "MB/s" (say "fast enough to stream" only if it's true)
- Pushy words: "hurry", "act now", "last chance", "don't miss out", "URGENT"
- ALL CAPS words, more than one exclamation mark, and emoji (they can break the message or cost extra)
- Text speak: "u", "ur", "gr8", "2nite"
- Words that sound like a scam: "winner", "congratulations", "claim your prize", "click here"

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **"Unlimited"** unless the same text says the fair-use limit, for example "Unlimited data, fair use 20 GB".
- **"Free"** if there are any conditions. Say what the condition is, or don't say "free".
- **"Fastest network"**, "best coverage", "No. 1" or anything else we can't prove.
- **"Cheapest"** or "lowest price anywhere". Say "low price" instead.
- **Hours that aren't true.** The Night Owl Bundle works from midnight to 7 am only. Never call it "all night", "24/7" or "any time".
- **Fake deadlines** or fake scarcity, like "only 10 left" or "ends tonight" when it doesn't.
- **Promises about speed** at a certain place or time.

## 5. How every message starts and ends

- **Start:** every text begins with our sender tag: `Chirpnet:`
- **End:** every text ends with the opt-out line: `Reply STOP to opt out.`
- Keep the whole text, including both lines, to 160 characters or fewer where possible.

**Example (about 125 characters):**

> Chirpnet: Up late? Get the Night Owl Bundle. Cheap data from midnight to 7 am. Reply OWL to add it. Reply STOP to opt out.
